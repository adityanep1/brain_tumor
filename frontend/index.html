How it works

PreprocessingImage is converted to grayscale, resized and normalised (z-score) so scans from different machines look alike.
U-Net segmentationAn encoder-decoder network with skip connections predicts a pixel-wise tumor mask.
ClassificationThe tumor region is cropped and a pretrained CNN predicts glioma, meningioma or pituitary.
Grad-CAMA heatmap of the classifier’s last conv layer shows which areas drove the decision.

Limitations: trained on a small set of T1 contrast-enhanced slices; works best on similar images; split by image, not by patient; not for clinical use.
